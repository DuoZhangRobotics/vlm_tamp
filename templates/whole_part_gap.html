Gap and tracking accuracy


The blue gap uses two references carried by independently fitted rigid parts. It is an estimate, not direct depth at the marked pixel. Missing values mean the current part pose could not be resolved. A good rigid fit does not certify that the chosen fixed reference means closed.


The native angle and reference-position error are evaluator-only measurements read after selection and tracking. They never enter the estimator or model prompt. Each pose is fitted directly to the registered part, without accumulating previous pose estimates.
