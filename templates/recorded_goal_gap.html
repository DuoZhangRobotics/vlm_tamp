Measured distance




The gap is the 3D distance between the two current tracked landmarks. Both positions come from measured depth at capture timestamps. There is no fixed-position substitution, invented depth, hinge sign, automatic point repair or alternate tracker. Gaps in the trace mean missing supported measurements.


A distance can be measured between incorrectly identified features. Finite depth and tracker visibility do not prove that the selected fixed point is the correct closed-position reference. Native door angles in the separate panel are evaluator-only labels, withheld from both models and the measurement code.
