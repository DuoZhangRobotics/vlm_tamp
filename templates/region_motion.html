Does combining the cameras help tracking?


All three conditions use the same selected regions and tracker outputs. “Both” combines their measured world correspondences in the existing rigid fitter. Single-camera conditions are comparisons; the system does not switch to whichever one looks better. Error is evaluated at the registered door-region centroid under native rigid motion. The registration capture is excluded from the error statistics and non-reference tracking count; fitting a capture to itself is not tracking success.
