Contact tools: caller choices and numerical execution

You choose the target, contact pixel, robot contact point and desired motion.
The tools convert calibrated RGB-D and robot offsets, solve cuRobo IK, and
execute timed references. They supply no task-specific action sequence.
Describe the desired part/reference relationship separately from the robot's
contact and motion. A surface normal gives a face orientation, not the direction
that achieves the task. A pivot locates a rotation axis, not a destination.
For an uncertain direction, a short probe followed by a fixed-view before/after
comparison is more informative than assuming a long hand stroke is task progress.

- push(observation_id, camera, contact_pixel_xy, contact_name,
  region_xyxy=null, target_region_xyxy=null, direction_world=null,
  distance_m=0.03, joint_degrees=null, joint_model_epoch=null,
  mode="controlled", contact_speed_m_s=0.03, contact_surface="fingertips",
  preview=false, budget_seconds=120, approach_wait_steps=20,
  contact_constraint="point", surface_normal_world=null): use the exact original-image integer
  pixel and a contact_name from reset's contact_landmarks_in_tcp. Select the
  observed part with a region or current part mask. No nearby pixel, wrist
  menu, stand-off or replacement contact is chosen. Point IK leaves wrist
  orientation free. For a selected observed face, contact_constraint="surface_normal"
  with its signed outward surface_normal_world aligns the chosen robot pad against
  that face while leaving twist free. This does not set direction_world or the goal.
  plan_contact_pose also accepts a full quaternion_xyzw when you choose one.
  VAMP plans the free-space approach, checking arm/palm and allowing all finger
  contacts. Planning and execution use the same aperture. preview issues no motion.
  Approach execution waits when measured joint lag exceeds the existing
  one-control increment (0.05 rad at 1 rad/s, 20 Hz). At the final goal it uses
  the existing measured-arrival tolerance: 4 mm and 0.05 rad orientation.
  Each reference can wait up to approach_wait_steps additional physics steps
  (default 20 = one second), ending early when it catches up. Every step counts
  against the episode. No target or IK solution is changed.
  approach_not_reached means the physical approach was attempted but did not
  arrive: the following push was NOT issued, and no contact is retained.
  Inspect the measured error and current observation before choosing a new
  explicit contact or orientation. A preview does not establish physical arrival.
  After measured approach arrival, the requested straight stroke starts at
  the actual hand pose. Arrival at the old target does not prove that the part
  stayed still or that contact was acquired. It uses Cartesian waypoints and cuRobo IK, with no push path planner,
  CPU IK refinement or extra dense collision pass. Physics resolves contact.
  Requested, commanded and measured motion are different; inspect each result.

- continue_push(direction_world, distance_m, contact_speed_m_s=null): reuse an
  existing retained contact/target. No scene rebuild or contact search. If no
  retained contact exists, this tool returns that fact; select push_stroke
  yourself if you want an unassociated hand motion.

- push_stroke(direction_world, distance_m, contact_speed_m_s=null): move the
  current hand directly through straight Cartesian IK targets. Only reset,
  direction and distance are needed. No target, hinge or contact is assumed;
  object motion is unmeasured. Hold the existing gripper aperture.

- inspect_video(start_control_step, end_control_step, max_frames=8,
  region_xyxy=null): inspect recorded agentview RGB over an executed interval.
  Returns a chronological contact sheet and individual original-resolution frames
  with control steps and simulated timestamps. Equal start/end requests one
  recorded frame; max_frames is 2..12 for interval sampling. A crop uses original
  image pixels. This reads existing live frames or the completed recording; it
  does not advance physics, capture new images, estimate a hinge or choose a
  task direction. Intermediate video frames have no new depth. Use current
  RGB-D observations, not resized video thumbnails, when selecting geometry.

- hook_pull uses the same explicit pixel/contact fields and an inward-facing
  pad on a visible retaining feature, without pinching. A flat selection has
  no retaining thickness. A small test motion measures engagement; unknown
  tracking is not evidence that a hook has engaged. Hook evidence is distinct
  from pinch-grasp evidence.

- plan_contact_pose(point_world_m, contact_name, quaternion_xyzw=null,
  contact_constraint="point", surface_normal_world=null) converts
  a measured point and chosen robot contact offset into cuRobo IK and a VAMP
  approach. An optional quaternion requests that orientation explicitly.
  Execute the returned plan with gripper=0 to keep the planned aperture.
  The surface-normal model and a full quaternion are alternative constraints.

Supply either direction_world or a signed increment of an explicitly associated
joint, including for previews. A surface normal does not determine task intent.
Positive joint motion follows the exported axis; it does not inherently mean
opening or closing. Geometry and motion measurements remain hypotheses/evidence.

Controlled direction pushes attempt the full requested hand distance (including
10 cm). stroke_length_m can explicitly divide it. Timing respects requested
contact speed and the existing 1 rad/s joint reference limit. Tracking failures
are reported. The stroke also waits for measured joint lag, and reports
push_reference_not_reached if it cannot follow a reference after one second.
Its final completion measures the selected contact point and, when requested,
the selected face normal; point-only IK leaves orientation free. This is physical feedback after attempting the
motion, not a scene-based rejection or a different motion plan. No force/tactile sensing is assumed.

push_and_release establishes slow contact, issues a bounded pulse and withdraws.
Requested speed may differ from achieved speed, and final object motion/rebound
is uncertain. Use observation and native success rather than a kinematic claim.

Initial search defaults to 30 s, later stroke IK to 5 s, and a skill to 120 s,
capped by the episode. Budgets limit work, not physical feasibility. An IK prefix
may execute if a whole physical push cannot be solved. Read reachable_prefix and
stop_reason. Fresh attempts re-estimate geometry; no successful coordinates or
state-specific triggers are supplied. Record your own successes, failures and
uncertainty separately from this tool description.

Motion replies separate hand travel, object-motion evidence and native task
success. Open feedback.visual_comparison.comparison_image: it pairs existing
before/after images from the same fixed cameras. Inspect the moving part relative
to the fixed task reference. A smaller projected silhouette alone is ambiguous,
and unknown object tracking does not validate a direction. These are evidence
tools, not required action sequences or motion gates.
