Contact tools: caller choices and numerical execution

You choose the target, contact pixel, robot contact point and desired motion.
The tools convert calibrated RGB-D and robot offsets, solve cuRobo IK, and
execute timed references. They supply no task-specific action sequence.

- push(observation_id, camera, contact_pixel_xy, contact_name,
  region_xyxy=null, target_region_xyxy=null, direction_world=null,
  distance_m=0.03, joint_degrees=null, joint_model_epoch=null,
  mode="controlled", contact_speed_m_s=0.03, contact_surface="fingertips",
  preview=false, budget_seconds=120): use the exact original-image integer
  pixel and a contact_name from reset's contact_landmarks_in_tcp. Select the
  observed part with a region or current part mask. No nearby pixel, wrist
  menu, stand-off or replacement contact is chosen. Without a supplied hand
  quaternion, cuRobo solves the chosen contact point with free wrist orientation.
  VAMP plans the free-space approach, checking arm/palm and allowing all finger
  contacts. Planning and execution use the same aperture. preview issues no motion.
  After approach, the requested straight stroke starts at the actual measured
  hand pose. It uses Cartesian waypoints and cuRobo IK, with no push path planner,
  CPU IK refinement or extra dense collision pass. Physics resolves contact.
  Requested, commanded and measured motion are different; inspect each result.

- continue_push(direction_world, distance_m, contact_speed_m_s=null): reuse an
  existing retained contact/target. No scene rebuild or contact search. If no
  retained contact exists, this tool returns that fact; select push_stroke
  yourself if you want an unassociated hand motion.

- push_stroke(direction_world, distance_m, contact_speed_m_s=null): move the
  current hand directly through straight Cartesian IK targets. Only reset,
  direction and distance are needed. No target, hinge or contact is assumed;
  object motion is unmeasured. Hold the existing gripper aperture.

- hook_pull uses the same explicit pixel/contact fields and an inward-facing
  pad on a visible retaining feature, without pinching. A flat selection has
  no retaining thickness. A small test motion measures engagement; unknown
  tracking is not evidence that a hook has engaged. Hook evidence is distinct
  from pinch-grasp evidence.

- plan_contact_pose(point_world_m, contact_name, quaternion_xyzw=null) converts
  a measured point and chosen robot contact offset into cuRobo IK and a VAMP
  approach. An optional quaternion requests that orientation explicitly.
  Execute the returned plan with gripper=0 to keep the planned aperture.

Supply either direction_world or a signed increment of an explicitly associated
joint, including for previews. A surface normal does not determine task intent.
Positive joint motion follows the exported axis; it does not inherently mean
opening or closing. Geometry and motion measurements remain hypotheses/evidence.

Controlled direction pushes attempt the full requested hand distance (including
10 cm). stroke_length_m can explicitly divide it. Timing respects requested
contact speed and the existing 1 rad/s joint reference limit. Tracking failures
and measured stalls are reported; they do not silently trigger new views,
corrective insertion or repeated strokes. No force/tactile sensing is assumed.

push_and_release establishes slow contact, issues a bounded pulse and withdraws.
Requested speed may differ from achieved speed, and final object motion/rebound
is uncertain. Use observation and native success rather than a kinematic claim.

Initial search defaults to 30 s, later stroke IK to 5 s, and a skill to 120 s,
capped by the episode. Budgets limit work, not physical feasibility. An IK prefix
may execute if a whole physical push cannot be solved. Read reachable_prefix and
stop_reason. Fresh attempts re-estimate geometry; no successful coordinates or
state-specific triggers are supplied. Record your own successes, failures and
uncertainty separately from this tool description.
