goal_logic describes ONLY the logical operators connecting symbolic goal predicates.
Use goal_logic="and" for a positive conjunctive goal set, including a single predicate.
Missing object-instance identity, uncertain localization, unknown joint axes, unknown grasp poses,
unresolved numerical parameters, and unexecuted actions do NOT change this logical operator.
Express those uncertainties through entity.reference_id, entity.ambiguous, parameters.status,
joint_type="unknown", and abstentions. All predictions remain verified=false.
Use goal_logic="unresolved" only when the instruction's logical goal structure cannot be represented
as a positive conjunction, such as an unresolved disjunction/negation. Explain that logical issue.
