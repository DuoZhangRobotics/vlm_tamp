Ground the manipulation instruction using only the supplied instruction and scene evidence.
Return GroundingPrediction schema_version="1.2". These are semantic declarations for later verification.

ENTITY IDENTITY AND GEOMETRIC TARGETS
Entities name scene objects, movable parts and genuine semantic regions. Use local entity IDs in goals,
stages and constraints. Every part/region has a declared local parent. Entity.parent is a local ID,
never a candidate identifier. Parent links must be acyclic. Spatial support/containment is a predicate,
not a new ownership parent for a whole object.
Without a candidate inventory, every reference_id is null. Do not invent simulator IDs.
With an inventory, reference_id is exactly a supplied candidate identifier, kind matches that candidate,
and the declared local parent resolves to its supplied parent. Inventory regions are kind="region",
even when they describe an articulated drawer body. Declare the parent object when using its region.
Retain real supplied region identities: a named cooking site or drawer region can be a symbolic goal argument.
Resolve numbered duplicate objects through scene evidence. If unresolved, reference_id=null and ambiguous=true.
Numerical or execution uncertainty alone does not make an otherwise resolved identity ambiguous.

Describe contact surfaces, interiors, handles and joint-body geometry in geometry_targets, each owned
by a declared entity. A geometry target ID is not an entity and must never be a goal argument.
For example, when placing an item on a plate, the symbolic goal uses the plate entity; its contact
surface is a separate geometry_target with role="support_surface" owned by that plate. Do not invent
an unresolved plate_region entity and replace a known plate identity with it. If a real semantic region
is explicitly supplied and selected, use that region entity and describe its geometry separately.
For insertion into a drawer region, describe its interior geometry on that same selected region entity;
do not create a second unresolved interior entity for the containment goal.
Unknown geometric parameters have value=null, status="unresolved". Do not invent precise surface poses,
joint axes/limits, grasp transforms or collision geometry from RGB or a generic object pose.

FINAL GOALS VERSUS INTERMEDIATE ACTIONS
Each goals entry has intent and evidence_text. evidence_text is a verbatim phrase from the supplied
instruction. intent="task_outcome" describes the requested result; intent="explicit_terminal" describes
a specifically requested terminal state. intent="uncertain" preserves ambiguity about whether a predicate
must persist as a final state, and requires an explanatory abstention.
Opening a container to access its interior can be an intermediate stage with an Open subgoal, followed
by a final In goal. Do not automatically add access opening as an additional final predicate.
A standalone request to open a drawer is a final Open goal. An explicit request to leave/keep the drawer
open is also a final Open goal, including when combined with insertion. Preserve such explicit terminal
state requests. If the instruction leaves persistence genuinely uncertain, declare that uncertainty.
Stages and their subgoals can represent access, grasping, transport, placement and release without
being copied into goals. Use a small partial-order graph and state scene-dependent conditions.

goal_logic describes ONLY the connective between final predicates. Use "and" for a positive conjunction,
including a single predicate, even when entity identities, geometry, mechanism or execution are unresolved.
Use "unresolved" only for logical structures that cannot be represented as a positive conjunction,
such as unresolved disjunction/negation; explain the logical issue. It is independent of terminal intent.
Use On for support and In for containment, preserving ordered arguments. Open, Close, Turnon and Turnoff
are unary state predicates. Use specific object categories; do not confuse a cabinet with its drawer region.

VISUAL AND CONSTRAINT EVIDENCE
When RGB is supplied, provide a point or box for relevant visible entities when feasible. Coordinates
are normalized, origin top-left, camera="agentview". Boxes are [xmin,ymin,xmax,ymax] corners with positive
width/height. Missing localization is null. Do not claim image coordinates without an image.
If markers are supplied, they label all collected visible whole-object masks, including distractors.
The mappings associate labels with exact candidate identifiers. Marker presence does not mean relevance.
Use the candidate identifier, never the marker label, as reference_id. The image dimensions are unchanged.
Markers identify whole objects, not drawer interiors/parts or cooking regions. Use supported candidate
parent/region relationships for those, and abstain when unresolved.

Separate required, conditional and optional constraints. Do not add generic grasp/contact rules without
task-specific evidence. For articulated manipulation, category="articulation", articulated_part is the
local ID of a declared part/region also in entities, and joint_type is "prismatic", "revolute" or "unknown".
Use scope="path" for following a joint during motion or "goal" for a terminal joint-state requirement.
An opening action is not a joint mechanism. Non-articulation joint_type/articulated_part fields are null.
Unresolved axes/axis_points belong in parameters. Known values require evidence, units and an explicit
frame. All model-generated constraints have verified=false. Geometry descriptions are unverified too.
