Role descriptions come from the user's instruction, not a scene inventory. Keep every qualifier in the phrase: color, left/right, and initial relations such as in a drawer or next to another object. Image points and boxes are normalized [0,1], top-left origin; use them for left/right and proximity after identifying the object types. They are not physical poses.
